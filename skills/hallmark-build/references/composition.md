# Composition choices

Choose one primary page shape from the content. These are prompts for judgment,
not required templates or a rotation game.

| Shape | Best fit | Avoid when |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Long document | explanations, policy, editorial narrative | the task is a quick transactional flow |
| Workbench | developer tools, editors, operations | there is no real product interaction to show |
| Catalogue | many comparable products, resources, or examples | one offer needs a clear narrative |
| Split studio | two complementary ideas or product + proof | the split creates duplicate hierarchy |
| Stat-led | verified quantitative evidence drives the decision | metrics are missing or speculative |
| Manifesto | a short, strong position is the product | users need detailed comparison or setup steps |
| Narrative workflow | a real sequence or transformation matters | the steps are invented marketing filler |
| Component playground | a library or API is best understood by trying it | the controls would be decorative only |
| Photographic | owned imagery carries meaning | imagery is unavailable or merely ornamental |
| Focused conversion | one clear offer and action | the product has several equal user goals |

## Compose the fingerprint

After choosing the shape, make five deliberate decisions:

1. **Entry:** statement, question, product interaction, evidence, image, or list.
2. **Hierarchy:** display face and body face roles, heading placement, and prose
   measure. Use at most one additional specialist face when content requires it.
3. **Navigation:** choose the smallest navigation model that represents the real
   information architecture: edge-aligned actions, compact product bar,
   masthead, side rail, search-led navigation, or no global nav.
4. **Section rhythm:** vary density, rules, paper shifts, image scale, or sticky
   relationships only when they clarify grouping. Equal whitespace is not the
   only separator, but asymmetry without meaning is decoration.
5. **Exit:** task completion, next step, newsletter, dense index, statement, or
   a minimal legal close. Do not add a four-column link footer to content that
   has no such destinations.

Pick color and motion after the structure. Brand colors and existing tokens are
authoritative. For greenfield work, use a restrained paper/ink/accent system and
one or two motion primitives rather than a named visual trend by default.
