# Bundled example prompts

The three images in this directory were generated independently with the same style core and four style-reference images. The references were explicitly labeled as style-only inputs so their architecture and geography would not be copied.

## Shared style core

```text
Construct the entire scene from crisp, tiny monospaced letters, digits, punctuation, and directional symbols aligned to one strict rectangular character grid. From a distance it must read as a coherent painterly scene; at close range individual glyphs remain clearly visible. Map glyph density to luminance and glyph direction to local contours. Use a restrained 8–12 color palette, atmospheric depth, matte vintage-print softness, and slightly faded reproduction colors. Do not create ordinary pixel art, a terminal UI, a conventional painting with text overlaid, meaningful sentences, logos, signatures, watermarks, or smooth non-glyph areas.
```

## Forest

```text
Create a square scene of a deep primeval forest: towering old trees frame a winding woodland path, layered ferns and moss lead toward a small bright clearing, with no people or buildings. Use vertical glyphs for trunks, directional marks for branches and the path, sparse punctuation for light and mist, and dense glyphs for foliage and shadows. Use deep pine and umber shadows, moss and dusty teal midtones, muted ochre sunlight, and warm ivory highlights. The mood is ancient, quiet, and contemplative.
```

Result: [`forest.png`](./forest.png)

## Snow night

```text
Create a square snow-night scene: a quiet snow-covered pine valley beneath a vast moonlit sky, a small wooden cabin beside a frozen stream with one warm window, and distant mountain silhouettes. Use sparse punctuation for stars and snow, horizontal marks for frozen water, slashes for ridges and roof planes, and vertical glyphs for pines. Use deep indigo, slate blue, icy blue-gray, moonlit cream, near-black blue, and one restrained amber accent. Avoid excessive glow and saturated electric blue.
```

Result: [`snow-night.png`](./snow-night.png)

## Golden mountain

```text
Create a square sunrise scene of a monumental snow mountain receiving first light: the summit and upper ridges glow gold while the valley remains cool and shadowed, with dark forested foothills and a winding river in the foreground. Use slashes and backslashes along mountain planes, dashes for clouds and reflections, vertical glyphs for forest, and dense glyphs for shaded foothills. Use powder and slate blue, glacier ivory, warm gold, muted ochre, subtle rose alpenglow, and deep olive-umber. Concentrate the gold on the summit instead of spreading it across the whole image.
```

Result: [`golden-mountain.png`](./golden-mountain.png)
